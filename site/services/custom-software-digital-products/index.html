Software and websites built around how you work

Ideal Technology Solutions designs, builds and supports the applications, websites and portals that Metro Detroit organizations rely on every day.

Book a 30 minute call

Tools that fit your process

Most organizations reach a point where spreadsheets, email threads and off the shelf tools stop keeping up. Staff type the same information into several places. Customers or donors can't find what they need on your website. We build software around the way your team already works, so people spend their day on the job instead of on workarounds.

ITS has served businesses in Metro Detroit since 2001, from our headquarters in Southfield, Michigan. We start by learning how the work actually gets done, then build to that. After launch, the same team stays with you to support and improve what we built.

We build on Microsoft's .NET platform and the Azure cloud, keep your data in Azure SQL, and use React and Angular for the screens your staff and customers use. We build websites on WordPress or on a custom content management system (CMS), depending on what each organization needs, so your team can update them without help.

Software projects rarely need us in the room, so we also build for clients in the Atlanta area and across the United States.

What we build

  • Custom Software & Business Applications

    Internal applications that replace manual tracking, approvals and reporting. We can set up sign in with your staff's existing Microsoft accounts, control who sees what, and record every change. We also modernize older systems that still hold important data but have become hard to maintain.

  • Website Design & Development

    Your website is often where a customer, donor or parent decides whether to trust you. We design sites that load quickly, work on any screen and are structured so search engines can read them. Each site is built on a CMS, so your team can update pages, news and events themselves. For hosting and protection, our Cloud & Cybersecurity team keeps your site secure and backed up.

  • Mobile Apps

    Apps for phones and tablets that give customers, members or field staff what they need away from a desk, such as checking a status or submitting a request. We connect each app to the systems you already run, so information is entered once and appears everywhere it is needed.

  • Business Portals & System Integration

    A secure portal gives customers, partners or members one place to sign in and see their own accounts, documents and requests. Behind the scenes, we connect your existing systems through application programming interfaces (APIs), so your accounting, customer relationship management (CRM) and operations data stay in step. Once your data is connected, it can feed the reporting described on our Data, AI & Automation page.

    accounting CRM operations APIs

  • UX/UI Design & MVP Development

    Good software starts with how people will use it. Our user experience and user interface (UX/UI) design work maps out screens and steps with your staff before any code is written. For a new product or service, we can build a minimum viable product (MVP): a smaller first version that does the essential job, so you can put it in front of real users and decide what to build next.

How we work with you

Every engagement starts with a conversation about your goals, your users and your budget. We don't publish fixed prices, because the right build depends on what you need. Instead, we quote each project after that first conversation. Once your software is live, our support team responds to requests within one business day, and across Metro Detroit we visit in person when a problem can't be solved remotely.
