Start with the data you already have

Most businesses collect more data than they use. It sits in accounting software, spreadsheets, Microsoft 365 and the apps your staff open every day. Getting a simple answer often means hours of copying, pasting and checking.

We help you bring that data together and see it clearly. Then we remove the manual steps around it. We start with one report or one process, prove it works, and build from there.

Built on the Microsoft systems you already use

This work runs on Azure, Azure SQL, Power BI and .NET. It is the same stack we use for our custom applications and cloud projects. If your business already runs on Microsoft 365, new tools can sign in with the accounts your staff already have through Microsoft Entra ID and follow the permissions you already set. You do not need to replace your current systems to get started.

  1. Microsoft Entra ID
  2. Power BI
  3. Azure SQL
  4. your current systems

Power BI Dashboards & Business Intelligence

Leaders should be able to check how the business is doing without waiting for someone to build a spreadsheet. We design Power BI dashboards around the questions you ask most often.

  • Dashboards for sales, operations, finance, enrollment or grant reporting
  • Reports that refresh on a schedule instead of being rebuilt by hand
  • Shared definitions, so every department works from the same number
  • Role based access control, so each person sees only the data meant for them

Data Integration

A dashboard is only as reliable as the data underneath it. We connect the systems you run today and organize their data in one place.

  • Connections between accounting, customer, inventory and Microsoft 365 data
  • A central Azure SQL database that serves as one trusted source
  • Cleanup of duplicate and inconsistent records
  • Links built through application programming interfaces (APIs) where your software provides them

Workflow & Process Automation

Every office has tasks that follow the same steps each time. Automating them frees your staff for work that needs judgment.

  • Approval routing for requests, invoices and purchase orders
  • Alerts when a record needs attention or a deadline is near
  • Moving data between systems so nobody retypes it
  • A log of every automated step, showing what ran and when

AI Applications & Chatbots

AI works best on specific, well defined jobs. Our rule is simple: AI drafts and sorts, and a person reviews and decides. We will tell you plainly when a task is a poor fit for AI.

  1. AI drafts and sorts
  2. a person reviews and decides
  • Chatbots that answer common questions using only documents you approve
  • First drafts of replies, summaries and reports for staff to edit
  • Sorting incoming forms and emails so they reach the right person
  • Pulling key details out of documents and into your systems

The goal is to give your team time back and fewer errors to fix.

Keeping sensitive data private

Healthcare providers, public agencies and K-12 schools hold information that must stay private. Before any AI tool touches that information, we agree with you on what the tool can see, where the data is stored, and who reviews the results. Tools can be limited to approved sources, and every action can be logged. Your compliance and legal staff make the final call, and we build to their requirements. Read more about how we protect data on our Cloud & Cybersecurity page.
