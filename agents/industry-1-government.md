## Keeping offices and public services running

A clerk's counter, a treasurer's office and a public works yard all depend on the same computers and network. When something fails, residents notice first. ITS manages that equipment so your staff can stay focused on the public.

Automated systems monitor your computers and servers every day, weekends included, and send email alerts at any hour. Our support team responds to requests within one business day. If a problem can't be fixed remotely, a technician comes to your building anywhere in Metro Detroit. We also handle network design, Microsoft 365, device setup and hardware purchasing.

See how our [managed IT and infrastructure services](/services/managed-it-infrastructure/) work.

## Protecting resident data and public systems

Local governments hold tax records, permit files, utility accounts and personal details for thousands of residents. Public bodies are frequent targets for ransomware, and an attack can close an office for days.

ITS installs endpoint protection on every device and sets up backups you can actually restore from. Identity and access management, including multifactor authentication (MFA), controls who can reach sensitive records. Where it makes sense, we move workloads to the cloud and plan disaster recovery so a tax deadline or council meeting isn't lost to an outage.

Read more about our [cloud and cybersecurity services](/services/cloud-cybersecurity/).

## Better online services for residents

Residents want to pay a bill, request a permit or find a meeting agenda without calling city hall. ITS designs websites, resident portals and online forms that work well on a phone and are easy to read.

Our websites are built to WCAG AA accessibility standards, so people who use screen readers or keyboard navigation can use them too. Portals can connect to the systems your staff already work in, which means less retyping and fewer errors.

Learn more about our [custom software and digital products](/services/custom-software-digital-products/).

## Modernizing older systems on a public budget

Many public offices still rely on servers and software bought a decade or more ago. Replacing everything at once is rarely possible, and rarely wise.

We start with an audit of what you have today. From there, ITS builds a roadmap that phases upgrades across budget years, beginning with the systems that carry the most risk. We can size each phase to fit a single budget year.

Explore our [digital transformation and technology consulting](/services/digital-transformation-consulting/).
