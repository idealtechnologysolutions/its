# Task: Write the homepage

Write the ITS homepage. Follow CONTENT_AGENT.md and PROJECT_BRIEF.md as always. The five service pages are finished and attached to this chat. Use them as your source for what each service covers, and do not add anything they do not say.

## What the homepage must do

A visitor should understand three things within a few seconds: who ITS is, what it does, and why to trust it. Then every visitor should find a clear path to the page that fits them.

## Sections, in this order

1. **Hero.** A short headline and one sentence. Lead with Metro Detroit. Show that ITS covers both everyday IT and building software. One button: "Book a 30 minute call", linking to /contact/.
2. **Five services.** One card per pillar. Each card gets a short title, one or two sentences, and a link to its page slug. Describe each service the way its own page does, in fewer words. Do not copy sentences from the service pages.
3. **Who we work with.** The seven industries in priority order, each linked to its slug. One sentence for government and K-12 about written scopes of work, itemized quotes and planning around budget cycles.
4. **Selected work.** Three case studies: Sean Anderson Foundation, The Chemico Group and DPSCD Foundation. One or two sentences each, each from a different angle than the service pages used. Link each to its /work/ slug, plus a link to /work/.
5. **Why ITS.** Founded in 2001, 25 years supporting Metro Detroit, on site visits across Metro Detroit, response within one business day, a human reviews everything. Use only facts in the brief.
6. **Where we work.** Metro Detroit and Michigan first. Clients in the Atlanta area. Cloud and software work across the US. No office or on site claim outside Metro Detroit.
7. **Closing call to action.** Invite a 30 minute call with a client advisor. Same button text and link.

## Limits

- 500 to 750 words. The homepage summarizes; the service pages explain.
- No statistics, testimonials, certifications, partner names or prices.
- Do not mention Mexico, telephony or the 2012 milestone.
- Page meta, reviewer notes, cross links and suggested visuals as usual.
