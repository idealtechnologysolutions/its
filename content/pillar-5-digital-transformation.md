## When you know something has to change

Most owners can tell when their technology is slowing the business down. Staff wait on old computers. The same information gets typed into two systems. Nobody is quite sure which licenses are still in use, or which device would cause the most trouble if it failed tomorrow.

The difficult part is knowing where to begin. Each vendor recommends its own product, and the wrong first purchase can lock you in for years. We help you see the full picture and make decisions in an order that fits your budget.

## Advice and planning, kept separate from delivery

This service covers the thinking: reviews, plans and recommendations. Our other services cover the work itself. When your plan calls for action, each recommendation points to the team that can carry it out.

- Support, networks, devices and hardware: [Managed IT & Infrastructure](/services/managed-it-infrastructure/)
- Cloud moves, backup and security: [Cloud & Cybersecurity](/services/cloud-cybersecurity/)
- Websites, portals and custom applications: [Custom Software & Digital Products](/services/custom-software-digital-products/)

The plan is yours to act on. Many clients ask us to deliver it, and you are free to use your own team as well.

## How we work

### 1. We assess what you have

We meet your team, review your systems and learn how work actually moves through the business. Across Metro Detroit, we come on site. For clients in the Atlanta area and elsewhere in the US, much of the review can be done remotely.

### 2. We recommend a plan in writing

You receive a written report covering what we found and what we recommend, with an estimated cost range for each step and a firm quote to follow once the work is scoped. Items are ranked by risk and business impact, so the most urgent work sits at the top.

### 3. You decide what happens next

You choose what to act on and who does the work. Some clients ask us to deliver part of the plan. Others give it to their own team. In both cases you have a clear document to budget against.

## What we help you plan

### Technology Assessments & IT Audits

A structured review of your hardware, software, network, licenses, backups and security settings. We note what is aging, what is unsupported and where a failure would hurt most. The result is an honest baseline written in plain language.

### Technology Roadmaps & Strategy

A roadmap turns findings into a schedule. We map changes over the next one to three years, tied to your business plans and budget, so purchases happen in a sensible order. Strategy work also covers larger questions, such as when to move systems to the cloud or replace software that no longer fits.

### Process Improvement

Many delays come from the workflow itself. We follow a task from start to finish, such as an order, an invoice or a service request, and look for repeated data entry, paper steps and approvals that stall. Where automation would help, the work moves to our [Data, AI & Automation](/services/data-ai-automation/) team.

### System Modernization Planning

Older systems often still do an important job. We help you decide whether to keep, upgrade, replace or rebuild each one, and what that change will mean for your staff and your data. New applications are built by [Custom Software & Digital Products](/services/custom-software-digital-products/). Moves to the cloud are handled by [Cloud & Cybersecurity](/services/cloud-cybersecurity/).

### Connected Technology

Security cameras, door access systems, sensors and building controls now share the same network as your computers. These devices are often called the Internet of Things (IoT). We list what is connected, check how each device is set up, and recommend how to keep it apart from sensitive data. Network changes are delivered through [Managed IT & Infrastructure](/services/managed-it-infrastructure/).
