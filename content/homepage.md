## Why ITS

We opened in 2001, supporting Windows, Novell, Cisco and other platforms across the Detroit metropolitan area. By 2004 we were also selling hardware and software directly. Today our headquarters is in [Southfield, Michigan](/about/).

Our monitoring systems check your computers and servers all day, every day, weekends included. They send email alerts at any hour. Our support team responds to requests within one business day. When an issue needs someone on site, we visit you anywhere in Metro Detroit.

We don't publish a price list. We quote every engagement after we talk through what you need.

## Where we work

Metro Detroit and Michigan are home. Our team is local, and our on site visits cover all of Metro Detroit.

We also work with clients in the Atlanta area. For cloud and software projects that don't need someone in the room, we serve organizations across the United States.
