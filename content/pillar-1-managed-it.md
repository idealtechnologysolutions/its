## One team for the technology you rely on

Most small and medium sized organizations don't have an IT department. A manager or office administrator ends up fielding password resets and chasing vendors. That time comes straight out of their real job.

ITS gives you a team that knows your setup. We keep your systems working day to day and plan upgrades with you, so equipment gets replaced on schedule instead of after it fails.

## Managed IT Services

Our monitoring tools check your computers and servers all day, every day, weekends included. If something looks wrong, such as a drive running out of space or a server going offline, the system sends an email alert right away, at any hour. Our team picks up those alerts during business hours and fixes the cause, often before it disrupts your team.

Each client gets a structured service plan that spells out what we manage. We also track the age of your equipment and software, so you can budget for replacements well ahead of time.

- Automated monitoring of computers and servers, seven days a week
- Email alerts sent at any hour
- Structured service plans with a clear scope
- Technology lifecycle management for computers, servers and software
- Infrastructure modernization for older systems that slow your team down

Security, backup and cloud services are covered on our Cloud & Cybersecurity page.

## IT Support

When someone on your team needs help, they contact us and hear back within one business day. We start by working on the problem remotely. If the fix needs hands on the equipment, we send someone to your location anywhere in Metro Detroit.

We also set up new computers and devices, so they are ready to use when they reach your staff.

## Network Design

A slow or patched together network costs your team time every day. We design wired and wireless networks for offices, warehouses and plants, including organizations with more than one location. Firewalls and secure remote access for staff working from home are part of the design from the start.

We plan for growth too. Adding staff, a floor or a new site shouldn't mean rebuilding from scratch. As more equipment connects to your network, from security cameras to sensors on a production line, we plan how those devices join it and keep them apart from the systems that hold sensitive data.

## Hardware & Software Sales

We have sold the equipment and software businesses run on since 2004. Here is what we supply:

- Laptops, desktops and workstations
- Servers and storage
- Routers, switches, firewalls and wireless equipment

Before you buy, we check that a product fits what you already have and where you are headed. After you buy, we install and configure it. We also handle procurement and licensing, so quotes, orders and renewals come from one place.

## Microsoft 365 & Licensing

Microsoft 365 is where many teams keep their email, files and meetings. We set it up for your organization, add and remove users as staff come and go, and match each person to the right license. Getting that mix right keeps you from paying for seats nobody uses.

## For government and K-12 buyers

For schools, we schedule work around the academic calendar so major upgrades happen while classrooms are empty.
