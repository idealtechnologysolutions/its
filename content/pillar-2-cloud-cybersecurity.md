## Cloud Infrastructure & Microsoft Azure

The cloud lets your team reach files and applications from the office, from home or on the road. We design and deploy Microsoft cloud environments, with Azure as our main platform. Each move is planned in stages so daily work keeps running while systems change over.

- Cloud infrastructure design and setup
- Microsoft cloud deployments
- Migration of servers, files and applications to Azure
- Ongoing cloud operations
- DevOps support for teams that build and release their own software

## Cybersecurity & Endpoint Protection

Every laptop, desktop and server is a way into your business. We supply and configure security software that guards each device. We also manage the licensing, so protection doesn't quietly lapse when a renewal date passes.

- Endpoint protection for computers and servers
- Security software selection, setup and licensing
- Advanced cybersecurity services matched to your level of risk

Automated monitoring of your computers and servers is part of our Managed IT & Infrastructure service. It runs every day of the week and sends alerts by email at any hour. Our support team responds within one business day.

## Identity & Access Management

Stolen passwords are one of the most common ways into a business. We use Microsoft Entra ID to control who can sign in and what each person can open once they do.

- Single sign on (SSO) across Microsoft and business applications
- Multifactor authentication (MFA) that asks for a second check
- Role based access control (RBAC), so staff see only what their job requires
- Prompt removal of access when someone leaves

## Backup & Disaster Recovery

A failed server or a ransomware attack can stop your business for days if your data has no safe copy. We set up backups of your files and systems and test that they can be restored. We also write recovery plans that set out which systems come back first and who does what during an outage.

## Secure Application Deployment

Business applications often hold your most sensitive information. When we deploy one for you, security is part of the setup from the first day. That includes controlled sign in, access by role, protected data connections and records of important changes. If you need a new application built, see our Custom Software & Digital Products page.

## For healthcare, government and K-12

Patient records, resident data and student information call for extra care. We help healthcare providers, public agencies and schools meet the data protection requirements that apply to them, such as HIPAA for healthcare. Our role is to put the right technical controls in place and document them clearly for your own review. Government and school clients receive written scopes of work and itemized quotes, and we plan projects around budget cycles and the school calendar.

## The Chemico Group: Credit Hold Repository

The Chemico Group, a manufacturer, needed an internal application to track credit holds as they move through a five stage resolution scale. We built it and hosted it on Microsoft Azure. Staff sign in with their Microsoft Entra ID work accounts, so the company manages access in one place and can shut it off when someone leaves. Every edit is logged, which gives Chemico a full history of changes to review at any time.
